# INTL/CEDEAOJuricaf — ECOWAS Community Court of Justice (Juricaf)

Full-text decisions of the **ECOWAS Community Court of Justice** (Cour de
justice de la Communauté économique des États de l'Afrique de l'Ouest,
CEDEAO) — the judicial organ of ECOWAS, with jurisdiction over Community
law and human-rights complaints across its West African member states —
as published on [Juricaf](https://juricaf.org) by AHJUCAF.

- **Data type:** case_law
- **Language:** French (judgments are often trilingual EN/FR/PT)
- **Access:** Juricaf JSON search API (`facet_pays:CEDEAO`) → per-decision HTML
  page (`/arret/{id}`), full judgment text extracted from `div#textArret`.
- **Corpus:** ~113 decisions.

A reachable full-text alternative to **INTL/ECOWASCJ** (africanlii.org), whose
Laws.Africa/PeachJam host is frequently residential-blocked.
